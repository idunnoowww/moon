page 03 / midnight entry

dear diary, i think i miss them again.

dear diary,

i think i miss them most in the little moments. when something funny happens and they're still the first person i want to tell, or when a song plays and somehow, it feels like it belongs to them.

sometimes i wonder if they ever think of me like that, too. if they ever come across something and wish i were there to see it. if they miss me in the quiet moments, when there's no one else around.

i hate how i still look for them in things, even when i know i can't always reach them.

i know they have their own world, their own thoughts, their own reasons for being distant. i've tried to understand that. i really have.

but sometimes, i wish i didn't have to be so understanding. sometimes, i just want to be missed. to be remembered without having to wonder if i still matter.

i don't know what i am to them anymore. i just know that, somehow, they still feel like home to a part of me that doesn't know where else to go.
